The current study

Does learning only the contact-rich phase generalize better than learning whole trajectories?

H1 · Generalization

The segment policy, composed with VLM-guided reaching, beats a whole-trajectory policy under spatial, object and visual shifts.

H2 · No loss in distribution

With the same demos, network and training steps, both are on par where they were trained.

H3 · The reason

The gain comes from modelling only the contact phase, not just from starting close. Control C1 tests this.

Contact-rich means grasp, insert, open, turn, wipe, place: the part a scripted controller cannot do. Free-space motion between contact phases is handled by the VLM and a planned reach.
