Interim: one seed

Seed 1 is training. Next: re-rendered views, which kept clean precision on LIBERO.

E5 · MimicGen, colour + texture augmentation (the recipe that passed the LIBERO gate) · seed 0, 100 episodes per bar

Same augmentation that made the LIBERO grasp skill robust, applied identically to whole-trajectory and segment policies. For whole-trajectory policies it is close to free robustness. For the wrist-only segment policy it hurts the precision insertions: with one camera at close range, colour randomisation removes the cues that align the part, while the agent-view policies have geometry from a second view. This bounds the contact-segment story: the segment policy needs appearance robustness that preserves precision, which is what physically consistent re-rendering gave on LIBERO. It also means the LIBERO choice cannot be transferred blindly across tasks.
