Reactive = three query styles on the external view (point, numbered marks, yes/no), then two re-asks from the wrist camera on the way in, with a vote. About 5 calls, 3 s of VLM time.

4B asked five times beats 32B asked once, at an eighth of the GPU memory.

When the three styles agree, grounding is 99% correct: a free uncertainty signal.

Franka in Isaac Lab · 257 test episodes per cell · close views get worse below ≈ 40 cm for small models; pooling votes beat “trust the last view”

Model size barely helps: 2B to 32B adds about 6 points of grounding. Pooling answers from different query styles helps more, because their mistakes are partly independent. A tested rule that let only the last unobscured close-up view decide was worse than asking once.
