In 83% of shuffled episodes the target sits where no demo ever grasped. Policies that must find the object grab whatever now occupies the old spot.

Held-out split (250 untouched episodes): ours 96.0% / 75.2%, end-to-end 97.6% / 0%.

Most robustness comes from the reach. The learned grasp pays off under localization noise (2–4 cm: 84–90% vs 41% scripted).

LIBERO proof of concept · all shuffled results are zero-shot (nothing trained or tuned on shuffled scenes)

Standard layouts are nearly fixed in LIBERO-Object, so every learned method looks perfect there. The shuffle moves the six grocery items between positions. SayCan-style and HAMSTER-style are controlled re-implementations with the same architecture, data and budget, not the original systems.
