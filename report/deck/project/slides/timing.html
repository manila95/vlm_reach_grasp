How long a pick takes, and how to make it faster

13 s

robot time per pick at today’s conservative speed; about 90% is the arm moving or waiting. The VLM takes 0.5–1.4 s.

7.5 s

at 3× speed with “wait until arrived”: same success (78.6% vs 78.2%). A short fixed wait drops to 69%.

≈ 4–5 s

estimated with time-optimal trajectories, targeted waits, no return home, and the VLM running during motion.

Bigger projects that also fix failure modes: GPU motion planning with collision checks (cuRobo), distilling the multi-query VLM into one fast model, and a learned closed-loop approach-and-grasp skill.

Franka in Isaac Lab · robot time = simulated motion, waits and gripper; 257 episodes per speed setting

With a short fixed wait, the joint controller lags fast motions, so the gripper closes before the arm arrives (median error 26 mm at 3×). Waiting until every joint is within 0.002 rad brings the error to 1.9 mm and keeps success at 1× levels.
