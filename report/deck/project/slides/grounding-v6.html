Correction. I first attributed the light and background basket errors to small items merging with the robot pedestal in the depth proposals; the self-filter fixes that merge, but it was not why the wrong proposal was chosen. LIBERO-Plus instruction strings carry generator metadata after the receptacle ("basket light 14", "basket table 15", "level1 sample3"), and parser v1 copied it into the grounding query. On the same scene, the VLM picks the true basket with a large margin when asked for "basket". v6's validated gain is the box centre: camera tasks, whose instructions are clean, went from 70 to 92% on the dev split. Parser v2 (grounding v7) asks for an identifying pick phrase and extracts the receptacle word; it was checked on the dev-split instructions only.
