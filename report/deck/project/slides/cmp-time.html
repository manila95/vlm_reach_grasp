What the better VLM costs: 16 s with Gemini, 9 s locally

Our policy is nearly free

0.14 s per episode (5 calls × 27 ms) vs 1.8 s for π0.5 (31 × 61 ms) and 4.2 s for MolmoAct2.

The VLM is the cost

Once per episode: Qwen-8B 1.2 s, Qwen-32B 3.0 s, Gemini Flash recursive 9.9 s (two API calls).

Shorter robot motion

5.9 s vs 7.1–7.4 s for the VLAs: the planned reach goes straight to the target.

Idle GPUs, 45 LIBERO-Plus dev tasks (every 12th), first server call excluded · inference assumed to block the robot · robot time = simulated steps / 20 Hz

How it was measured. Each system ran 45 tasks sampled evenly across the perturbation categories, with one model served at a time on an otherwise idle GPU. Policy time is the client round trip of every model call (for the VLAs including the server); our grasp policy is the diffusion model's inference per chunk. VLM grounding time is the mean per scene over 24 cached scenes (the first scene is dropped as warm-up); Gemini includes network latency, so it depends on the connection, not on our hardware. Robot motion is simulated time (steps at 20 Hz); the 10 settle steps are excluded. SmolVLA is averaged over all episodes since it has a single success. Success on the bars is from the held-out test split, not from these 45 tasks. π0-FAST is excluded (it does not run correctly on our GPUs). Real-robot speeds will differ; the point is the relative cost of perception and policy.
